Upload Apple Health data

To get your Active Energy data (from your Watch's Move ring):

  1. Open the Health app on your iPhone
  2. Tap your profile picture (top right) → Export All Health Data
  3. This produces a .zip file - transfer it here (AirDrop to your Mac, or however you get files off your phone)
  4. Upload that .zip directly - no need to unzip it first

Large exports (100MB+) may take a moment to process.
